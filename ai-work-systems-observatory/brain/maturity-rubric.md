# Operating-system maturity rubric

Maturity is assessed only after lifecycle classification. Available capabilities, incomplete setup, and delivered assets do not receive current-system maturity levels.

- **Level 0, manual pattern**: a repeatable business job is observed, but no recurring AI mechanism exists.
- **Level 1, available capability**: a reusable capability exists, but setup, owner, output, or use is incomplete.
- **Level 2, delivered mechanism**: a repeatable mechanism can execute or deliver, but consumption or ownership is unproven.
- **Level 3, qualified operating system**: owner, trigger, output, consumption, run health, and human control are evidenced.
- **Level 4, proactive owned system**: the system runs on cadence, maintains current context, reaches the intended audience, and produces repeated use evidence.
- **Level 5, governed learning loop**: outcome signals or owner-approved baselines inform the next cycle, with monitored quality, cost, and controls.

Do not call Level 2 or below a current operating system. Maturity describes operating structure, not effectiveness, productivity, or ROI.
