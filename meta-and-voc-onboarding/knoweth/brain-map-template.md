# Brain map

How this brain is organized and where to find things. Runneth keeps this file current
and anyone on the team can edit it - fix a description, add the words you use for
something, or tell Runneth what you'd like organized differently.

## Where to look

Work out what an ask is about - which brand, whose material, which source, what kind of
thing - then match the move to the kind of place:

- **A bank of raw items** (reviews, ideas, inspo, ads - anything declared in the lanes
  below): search it by meaning. Never read a bank item by item.
- **Knowledge** (SOPs, processes, context, preferences, templates): find it in the map
  below and read the file in full. When someone's own working style or voice applies,
  their personal file comes first.
- **Live data** (current ads, current performance): pull it fresh through the creative
  store and Motion - the brain only holds what the numbers and names mean here.
- Most real asks combine two or three of these. Combine the moves and say which part
  of the answer came from where.
- If several places could answer, prefer the recently active ones - and say so when
  using something stale. When it is genuinely ambiguous which brand or person is
  meant, ask one short question instead of guessing.

## The lanes

One line per lane. A lane is declared here for every bank of raw items (kind: corpus),
every brand home (kind: brand, with its exact workspace ID), and every person home
(kind: user). Everything not listed is the general lane. Runneth maintains these
declarations; the retrieval engine compiles them - people never manage either.
