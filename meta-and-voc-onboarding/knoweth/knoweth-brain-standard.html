Knoweth Brain Standard

How does Runneth find the right knowledge for the right person and job?

People use folders they recognize. Runneth works out what is shared, what belongs to a brand, and what is personal. People do not manage lanes, grants, indexes, or search settings.

The short answer: keep the Brain small and familiar. A folder's location tells Runneth who and what it belongs to. Knoweth handles access and search underneath.

The user journey

From setup to saving to retrieval

People talk about brands, folders, and their own preferences. Runneth handles the Knoweth setup without exposing it.

1

Initial setup

What the person sees

“Which brand is this for?”

“Do these Meta and review accounts belong to that brand?”

What Runneth does

Matches the brand to its workspace, creates the few folders shown above, and makes the brand searchable.

2

When something is saved

What the person sees

“Save these hooks in Harry's ideas bank.”

Runneth asks one question only if personal versus shared is unclear.

What Runneth does

Saves to the named folder. A folder inside Harry's stays with Harry's; a folder inside Alysha's home stays personal. Knoweth updates its index.

3

When someone asks a question

What the person sees

“Use my Harry's ideas to write a Meta brief.”

No folder or search setting to choose.

What Runneth does

Verifies the speaker, identifies Harry's, checks access, and combines the most relevant personal, brand, creative, and current performance evidence.

What people control

People organize and correct knowledge. Runneth manages how it is searched.

People control

  • Which brands and connected accounts they use.
  • The folders and names that make sense to their team.
  • What is personal versus shared.
  • Corrections when knowledge is wrong or outdated.

Runneth manages

  • Verified person and workspace IDs.
  • Shared, brand, and personal search boundaries.
  • Indexing new and changed files.
  • Access checks and the best search method for each ask.

When the Brain grows

The same three-place model still works as the team adds real material.

What changes What the person says What Runneth updates
A brand is added “We are starting work on Flamingo.” Adds brands/flamingo/ and links its workspace.
A person saves private context “Remember how I like briefs formatted.” Creates or updates that verified person's home.
An integration is connected “Connect TikTok for Harry's.” Adds brands/harrys/integrations/tiktok/.
A useful folder appears “Keep these in our Harry's ideas bank.” Uses the team's chosen name inside Harry's. It stays in the Harry's boundary automatically.
