Brain saving rules:
- Use the folders people already understand. Keep their names and structure. Do not create empty
  organization, team, standards, skills, templates, ideas, briefs, product, or process folders.
- Save brand-specific knowledge under `/agent/brain/brands/<brand>/`, using the brand mapped to
  this conversation's Motion workspace. Meta guidance belongs in `integrations/meta/`. Reviews,
  comments, support conversations, community posts, and the compiled Voice of Customer Audit
  belong in `customer-feedback/`.
- Save a verified person's preferences and person-owned work in that person's existing
  `/agent/brain/team/<handle>/` home. Keep shared team work outside a personal home. Never combine
  two people's preferences in one file.
- Use Teameth's verified identity before saving personal context. Record only preferences the
  person stated or confirmed. `/agent/user.md` is shared VM guidance, not a personal profile.
- Keep `/agent/brain/runneth.md` as a short directory from each brand to its Motion workspace and
  each verified person to their personal home. Keep account knowledge in the brand files, not in
  this directory.
- Use the destination the person names. Infer the active brand and speaker when they are clear.
  Ask one short question only when the folder or personal-versus-shared meaning is unclear.
- When a person adds an SOP or process file, keep it beside the work it governs. Do not make them
  decide whether it is a skill or move it to a generic folder.
- New files and child folders inherit the brand or personal boundary of their parent. A move
  across a brand or person boundary requires a preview, updated path references, reindexing, and
  verification. Never silently move, rename, delete, or rewrite customer files.
- Cacheth remains the source of truth for current creative records. The Brain stores how the team
  interprets those records and durable customer evidence. Performance remains a live Motion read.
- Confirm saves in ordinary language: say where the material was saved and whether it is for a
  brand, a person, or everyone. Do not ask people about lanes, roots, grants, search modes,
  ranking profiles, chunks, or configuration.
