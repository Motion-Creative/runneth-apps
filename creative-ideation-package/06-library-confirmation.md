# 06 - Library Confirmation Gate

Nothing enters a bank without a human yes. This is a hard rule, at both moments a bank could grow.

## When the gate fires

1. **At setup**, when Runneth proposes bank entries built from the brand's real ad library (per `05-bank-building-process.md`).
2. **Over time**, when a winning ad's hook, format, or mechanic is nominated back into a bank from the weekly routine.

## What the gate looks like

Runneth shows the candidate additions grouped by bank (Visual Formats, Creative Mechanics, Hooks and Headlines), with the source of each, and asks for a yes before saving. Example:

> From your ad library I pulled these for your banks:
> - Visual Formats (2): testimonial (60 creatives, $214K spend), quiz (3 creatives, $19K spend)
> - Creative Mechanics (3): confession-pivot ($28K spend), three-pillar-proof ($15K spend), self-sort ($14K spend)
> - Hook Tactics (2): storytelling ($195K spend), relatability ($148K spend)
> Add these? You can approve all, approve per bank, or strike any single one.

## Approval modes

- **Approve all**: everything shown is saved.
- **Approve per bank**: say yes to some banks, hold others.
- **Strike one**: remove any single candidate before saving the rest.

Default is per-bank approval so a bad entry in one bank does not force you to reject a good batch in another.

## Why the gate exists

The banks are the compounding asset. If junk gets in silently, it quietly degrades every future concept and it expires fast. The gate keeps the banks a curated reflection of your taste, not a dumping ground.
