# 01 - The Creative Flywheel (Scaffold)

The flywheel is the full loop a creative strategist runs. This package lights up one node, ideation, and leaves the rest as labeled slots you can fill later.

```
   Research  ->  Ideation  ->  Brief  ->  Production  ->  QA  ->  Launch  ->  Analysis
        ^              ^                                                           |
        |______________|___________________________________________________________|
```

| Stage | Status | What it owns |
|---|---|---|
| Research | Active input | Ongoing social listening, VoC audit, competitor scanning, and recent-winner review. Feeds micro-moments and grounds every concept in real customer language. Not a one-time upstream step. |
| **Ideation** | **Live** | Turning inputs into ad concepts. This is the engine. |
| Brief | Slot | Turning a concept into a production-ready brief. |
| Production | Slot | Making the asset. |
| QA | Slot | Checking the asset against the brief and brand rules. |
| Launch | Slot | Naming, shipping, and tracking. |
| Analysis | Slot | Reading results and feeding winners back into the banks. |

## Why ideation first

Ideation is the only stage with real taste in it. The others are process that most operators already have or can get anywhere. Ideation is where the judgment lives, so it is the thing worth capturing and the thing that makes Runneth feel like a strategist instead of a form.

## The loops that matter

Two loops are live:

1. **Research feeds Ideation.** The VoC audit and social listening surface real customer language (pain points, trigger moments, standout phrases) that become micro-moments in the engine. This is ongoing, not one-time.

2. **Analysis feeds the banks.** When an ad wins, its hook, format, or mechanic can be nominated back into the banks. A weekly routine proposes new entries from recent winners; the team approves before anything saves. That is what makes the engine compound instead of resetting every time.
