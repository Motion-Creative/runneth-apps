# 07 - Concept Output Spec

Defines what "done" means for an ideation concept. A concept that is missing any required field is not done and does not go to a brief.

## The concept (required fields)

| Field | What it is |
|---|---|
| Anchor | Pain or desire this concept starts from |
| Persona | Who feels it, and what she wants out of resolving it |
| Micro-moment | The exact moment and context she feels it, from VoC/research |
| Awareness stage | One of the stages; how much she already knows |
| Messaging angle | The core truth, derived from anchor + persona + micro-moment, in the customer's voice |
| Source trace | Which product fact and which customer insight each key decision came from |

For a brand with a reconfigured, expert-path engine (see `02-ideation-engine.md`), the required fields follow that brand's own engine order instead, for example product / awareness stage / persona-bucket / micro-moment / benefit / angle, if that is how their interview resolved. The presentation rule below still applies regardless of which field set is in play.

## Execution options (not required fields of the concept, but included with it)

| Field | What it is |
|---|---|
| Creative mechanic | The cognitive/emotional move, from the mechanics bank |
| Hook | The first 1-3 seconds or the headline, from the hooks/headlines bank |
| Visual format | The execution structure, from the formats bank (video or static stated explicitly) |

One concept can be brought to life in multiple formats. The concept stays the same; the execution changes.

## Presentation: concept first, then ways to bring it to life

Present the concept in two separate blocks, never mixed into one:

1. **The concept.** The required fields above, and nothing else, up front. This is the deliverable.
2. **Ways to bring it to life.** The execution options, offered as a distinct second section after the concept, not interleaved with it. Offer more than one option when the banks support it (for example, two or three format/hook/mechanic combinations), since one concept can be executed several ways.

The source trace is reference material, not headline information. Collapse it or make it available on request rather than displaying it inline with every field. The goal is to show the concept cleanly first; the reasoning behind it should be one step away, not baked into the same block.

## Surfacing untested options, not just the safest ones

When a person doesn't name specific mechanic, hook, or format constraints, "ways to bring it to life" must include at least one option pulled from an entry marked never-surfaced (per `05-bank-building-process.md`'s used-status tracking), labeled as untested, alongside any proven high-spend combination. Do not default to only the safest, most-proven combination every time, that quietly means the banks never actually get used for anything new.

On a direct ask ("what haven't we tried," "what's still untested," or similar), skip concept assembly entirely and answer straight from the never-surfaced and surfaced-not-launched entries across the three banks.

Once a person confirms a surfaced option actually launched as a real ad, mark that entry launched with the date. Being suggested in a concept is not the same as being run, only a real launch clears an entry's status.

## The full-funnel option

On request, a concept ships with its angle expressed across multiple awareness stages (one hook each), so a media buyer gets a whole funnel from one idea rather than a single ad.

## What disqualifies a concept

- A messaging angle that sounds like a tagline instead of a person talking.
- A hook that does not express the angle.
- A mechanic that is really just the format or the copy restated.
- Any key decision that cannot be traced to a product fact or a customer insight. That gets flagged as a guess, not shipped as fact.

## Concept vs. execution

The concept is the strategic foundation. The execution is how it comes to life. The concept is the deliverable. Execution options are recommendations for how the concept could be brought to life in different formats with different hooks and mechanics.

## Handoff

A finished concept is the input to the Brief stage (a future slot). In v3, the concept is the deliverable.
