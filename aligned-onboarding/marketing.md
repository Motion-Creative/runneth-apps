## Super powers this unlocks

- Rankings that match your logic. Runneth reads results against your north-star event and your targets, not a default metric it guessed.
- Fewer wrong reads. It learns the metrics that mislead on this account (blended ROAS, a broken column, a cryptic name) and what to use instead.
- A living creative library. One record per active creative with its hook, transcript, value props, and tags, kept current as new ads launch.
- Answers you can trust on day one. A person confirms the interpretation before Runneth relies on it, so nothing runs on a guess.

## How it works

Runneth auto-pulls a year of your Meta account, proposes how it reads the account across nine areas, then walks a short confirmation with someone on the team before saving anything. Once that lens is in place, it builds an enriched file per creative and reads the lens before every performance question from then on.

## A real example

Priya runs paid at a supplement brand where every product shares one pixel, so blended ROAS always looks weak. She installs Aligned Onboarding and spends ten minutes confirming what Runneth pulled: judge on cost per purchase, ignore ROAS, treat the marketplace campaign as off-Meta. A week later she asks "what are our best ads right now," and Runneth ranks by cost per purchase, leaves the marketplace campaign out, and points at the two creatives actually carrying spend. No caveats to correct, no metric to re-explain.
