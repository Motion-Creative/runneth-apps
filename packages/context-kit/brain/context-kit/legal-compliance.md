## What belongs here
The claims, words, and framings your brand is allowed and not allowed to make in ads and copy,
so creative clears review on the first pass.

## Thought starters
- Any claims you legally cannot make? (e.g. "clinically proven", "#1", "cures", "guaranteed")
- Required disclaimers or fine print on specific claims?
- Regulated categories that apply to you? (health, finance, beauty, supplements, kids)
- Words or comparisons legal has flagged before?
- Anything you must always attribute or cite?
